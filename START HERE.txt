START HERE
==========

HOW TO OPEN IT:
Double-click index.html. It opens straight in your browser — no server,
no install, nothing to set up. An internet connection is used only for
the decorative fonts; offline, the page falls back to system fonts and
everything else still works exactly the same.

THE OPENING:
There is a short cinematic opening (aurora, fireworks, HAPPY BIRTHDAY,
a photo moment, flowers, then the cake). You can skip it any time with
the SKIP button in the corner, or the Escape key.

THE CAKE IS INTERACTIVE:
Tap each candle to blow it out, then tap the cake to cut it.

CHANGING THE SKY:
The ☀ button in the top right opens "Choose Your Sky" — nine Aurora
themes, each with its own colours and atmosphere.

CUSTOMISING:
All the words, the memories and the themes are in js/config.js.
See README.md for details.
